Your pipeline, your call

Any rig system. Or a new one.

We don’t ask studios to adopt our tools. We rig in whatever your show already runs on, build by hand when that’s the better call, or design a system around your needs.

01

Your system

Already on mGear, an in-house autorig or another framework? We work inside it, follow its conventions, and the rigs look like your rigs.

02

Built by hand

Some characters are better rigged from scratch. Hero faces, odd anatomy, one-off props: bespoke setups when a template would cost more than it saves.

03

A new system

Need an autorig or rigging framework of your own? We design and build one around your shows, engine and naming, and hand it over documented.

04

Our framework

When the choice is ours, we often reach for Mutant Tools, our own modular framework for Maya. Fast for creatures and game rigs — and optional.

Process

How a job runs

No surprises at delivery. Scope and acceptance are agreed before a joint is placed.

01

Scope

You send the asset list, target engine, deadline and conventions. We come back with what we’d do first, the risks, and whether the date is real.

02

Definition of done

What a delivered rig includes, which conventions it follows, how many revision rounds. Agreed in writing before anyone opens Maya.

03

Blocking first

Rough rigs early so animation can start, then deformation passes, then polish. Problems surface while they’re cheap.

04

Validated delivery

Every asset goes through the same publish checks before it reaches you. Failures are loud and specific.

05

Handover

Rig documentation and a walkthrough call. Our work is meant to outlive the contract.
