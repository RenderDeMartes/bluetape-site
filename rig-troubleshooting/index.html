Rig rescue

Rig rescue for running productions.

Deformation failed review, the export broke, or the person who built the rig has left. We find what’s actually wrong and fix it without restarting the asset, unless restarting is genuinely cheaper.

What you get

What we deliver

  • Diagnosis of what’s actually breaking, not just the symptom
  • Deformation and skinning repairs
  • Export and engine import fixes
  • Stabilising rigs already in animation without breaking shots
  • A written account of what was wrong and what changed
  • Handover notes so your team can maintain it
How we approach it

Diagnostic first

The first pass is finding the cause, not polishing the surface. A deformation problem might be skinning, a bad corrective or a hierarchy issue; an export failure might be one joint’s orientation. We fix the cause so it stays fixed.

Without breaking animation

Rigs mid-production already have shots animated on them. We change what needs changing while keeping existing animation working, and tell you up front if something can’t be kept.

Any system, any author

Rigs built in-house, in mGear, in another framework or by hand. Picking up someone else’s rig is a normal job for us.

A good fit when
  • A rig failed review and the deadline hasn’t moved
  • Exports to the engine have started failing
  • The rig’s author has left and nobody knows how it works
  • Deformation breaks in poses the show now needs
FAQ

Questions studios ask

Can you fix a rig somebody else built?

Yes. That’s the normal case for rig rescue, whatever system it was built in.

Will fixing the rig break existing animation?

We work to keep existing animation working, and tell you up front if a fix can’t preserve it.

Do we get documentation of the fix?

Yes: a written account of what was wrong, what changed, and how to maintain it.
