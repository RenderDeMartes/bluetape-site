How we approach it

Built around your shows

A rigging system should encode how your studio rigs: its naming, its control shapes, its engine targets. We start from the characters you actually make and design the system around them.

Extend before you replace

Often the best move is not a new autorig but better tools around the one you have. We’ll tell you which is cheaper for your situation.

Yours when we leave

Everything is written to your coding standards, in Python and PySide/Qt, and documented so your team owns it after the contract. We also maintain our own open-source framework, Mutant Tools, so we know what it takes to keep a rigging system alive.

A good fit when
  • Your riggers repeat the same setup work on every character
  • Your current autorig can’t handle creatures or game rigs
  • You need publish checks so broken rigs stop reaching animation
  • You want a rigging system your own team can extend
FAQ

Questions studios ask

Can you build an autorig for our studio?

Yes. We design and build autorigs and modular rigging frameworks for Maya around your characters, naming and engine targets.

Can you improve our existing rigging tools instead?

Yes, and it’s often cheaper. We extend and adapt existing systems as well as building new ones.

Who owns the code?

You do, written to your standards and documented for your team.
