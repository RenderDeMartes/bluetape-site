How we approach it

Outsourcing without the clean-up afterwards

The risk with outsourced rigging is getting back rigs that work but don’t belong in your pipeline: wrong names, wrong hierarchy, controls your animators have to relearn. We start from your conventions, so the rigs we deliver look like the ones your own team builds.

Whatever system you rig with

We work inside your in-house autorig, mGear or another framework, build by hand when that’s the better call, or use our own tools when the choice is ours. The system is your decision; the deliverable is the same.

Scoped before anyone opens Maya

You send the asset list, the target engine or renderer, the deadline and an example of your conventions. We come back with what we’d do first, the risks we see, and whether the date is realistic, including telling you when we’re not the right studio for it.

FAQ

Questions studios ask

How does rigging outsourcing work with Bluetape?

You send the asset list, engine or renderer, deadline and your conventions. We agree scope, deliverables and revision rounds in writing, deliver rough rigs early so animation can start, then refine and hand over with documentation.

Can you work under NDA?

Yes, as standard. Most of our work sits behind another studio’s name.

Do you work remotely?

Yes. We work remotely with studios worldwide across animation, VFX and games.
