How we approach it

Your system, not ours

We have our own rigging framework, but when a show is built in mGear the right answer is mGear. Rigs that match the rest of the production are worth more than rigs built our way.

Rebuildable, not patched

mGear’s strength is rebuilding the rig from guides. Custom work goes into build steps and scripts rather than hand edits on the output, so a guide change doesn’t throw away days of fixes.

Production support

On Nicktoons & The Dice of Destiny we provided mGear rigging and production support for Fair Play Labs, working alongside the existing team.

FAQ

Questions studios ask

Do you only work with mGear?

No. We rig in mGear, other frameworks and in-house autorigs, by hand, or with our own tools. For mGear productions, we stay in mGear.

Can you add custom steps to our mGear build?

Yes. Custom work goes into build steps and scripts so rigs still rebuild cleanly from guides.

Have you shipped mGear rigs in production?

Yes, including rigging and production support on Nicktoons & The Dice of Destiny for Fair Play Labs.
